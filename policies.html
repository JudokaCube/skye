Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 3 October 2026. This policy covers data handled by the Skye bot and this website. The operator is judokacube. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, join the support server and open a ticket. Do not post personal information in a public support channel.

1. What the bot receives

Discord supplies information the bot is allowed to access: user, server, channel, message and role IDs; usernames and display names; avatars; membership and role information; command arguments and interactions; messages in accessible channels; and relevant server events. Presence data is available only when the operator enables Discord’s presence intent. The bot does not record voice audio.

Receiving an event does not mean all of its contents are permanently stored. Message content is processed to run commands and configured features such as filtering, counting, automatic responses, XP and AFK notices. The Discord client also maintains temporary in-memory caches. Skye cannot read arbitrary private conversations between other users.

2. Data stored for features

  • Configuration and access: server preferences, enabled modules, channel and role IDs, panel text, custom aliases/responses, security rules, exemptions, and saved permission states.
  • Tickets and voice rooms: channel IDs, owner IDs, ticket panel/support-role references, temporary-room ownership and configuration. Ticket conversation content stays in Discord; Skye does not create a separate transcript archive.
  • Moderation and membership: warnings with reasons, moderator IDs and timestamps; temporary-ban expiry records; moderation counters; and join, leave, nickname and role-change history. Membership history is recorded independently of whether optional event logging is enabled.
  • Community activity: balances, reward cooldowns, XP, reminder text and due times, AFK reasons/timestamps, giveaway entries and winners, counting state, bump-reminder state, and booster-role ownership/sharing.
  • Confessions: author ID, confession number and posted message ID for abuse investigation. The posted confession itself remains in Discord. The local record is not a promise of anonymity from the operator.
  • Bug reports: report text, reporter ID, server context, report ID and timestamp. Reports may be forwarded to the operator’s Discord messages and a configured report channel, including channel context.
  • Deleted messages: the snipe feature temporarily retains author information, a bounded content excerpt, deletion time and an attachment URL when present. Command messages are excluded from this cache.

3. Why data is used and who can see it

Data is used to deliver requested or administrator-configured features, enforce permissions, operate moderation and anti-abuse controls, investigate reports, and maintain the service. We do not sell or license Discord user data, use it for advertising, or use message content to train AI models.

Command responses and feature output are often visible in their Discord channel. Examples include leaderboards, AFK notices, reminders and polls. Ticket participants, support staff and administrators may see ticket content. Authorized moderators can retrieve recent snipes or membership history. Event logging can send metadata and moderation/security information to configured channels; message edit/delete logs do not copy message bodies, and command messages are excluded.

The operator can access locally stored data and reports for operation and support. Discord processes bot interactions, messages and attachments under its privacy policy. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address abuse, security incidents or the protection of legal rights.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing requested features is used to provide the service, and proportionate security, abuse prevention and support processing is based on legitimate interests. Legal obligations or consent may apply in specific circumstances. You may ask the operator about the basis relevant to your situation and exercise applicable rights below.

4. Third-party requests

  • Translation: text and language choices are sent to MyMemory / Translated. An operator-configured contact email may accompany requests. Do not translate confidential or sensitive text.
  • Weather: a location query is sent to Open-Meteo for geocoding, followed by a weather request for the resulting coordinates.
  • Definitions: the requested word is sent to Free Dictionary API.
  • Reaction images: an action category is requested from nekos.best; the lookup does not require sending the mentioned member’s ID. Discord may load the returned media.
  • Images: uploaded images and supported Discord CDN links are downloaded and validated for the requested profile or server customization, then sent to Discord.

These providers receive the request data and network metadata such as the bot host’s IP address. Their handling is governed by their own policies. Skye’s deletion of local data does not automatically delete information already processed by Discord or an external provider. Services may process data outside your country; their policies describe their arrangements.

5. Retention and deletion

Temporary memory: snipe records are limited to 10 per channel, expire after one hour and are periodically removed; a bot restart clears them. Authorized moderators can clear a channel’s snipe cache. Short-lived game and rate-limit state expires, is pruned or is cleared on restart.

Bounded records: the local database retains up to 100 bug reports and 200 confession-author mappings per server, and up to 25 membership-history events per user across at most 1,000 stored users per server. These are count limits, not guaranteed time-based deletion periods.

Persistent records: configuration, balances, XP, warnings, ownership and similar state remain until the relevant feature removes or replaces them, an authorized user/admin uses an available removal command, or the operator handles a request. Reminders are removed after delivery/cancellation, or after their delivery-retry period expires. There is no universal automatic deletion period. Disabling a module or removing Skye from a server does not automatically erase its saved database records.

Discord copies: bot responses, log messages, confession posts, reports and support tickets remain in Discord until deleted there under the relevant permissions and Discord’s handling. Clearing a local record does not erase screenshots, third-party copies or messages already posted. Diagnostic logs and any recovery copies are separate from feature records; ask support to include relevant operator-controlled copies in your request. We do not promise a backup-retention period that has not been established.

Use available feature controls to cancel reminders, remove your AFK status or stop participating. Server administrators can disable optional modules and remove the bot’s permissions or installation. For broader requests, join support and make a ticket.

In the ticket, say whether you want access, correction or deletion, provide your Discord user ID and relevant server ID(s), and describe the records involved. We may ask you to verify control of the account or your authority over server configuration. Never provide your password, bot token or account token. We will assess the request and respond without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines; limited retention may be necessary for legal obligations, security or unresolved abuse reports, and we will explain any restriction.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent where consent is used, and a complaint to your local data-protection authority. These rights are not all absolute. Server moderation disputes should also be raised with that server’s administrators; only the operator can address Skye’s separate local records.

Skye uses permission checks, bounded processing and restricted local file access to reduce risk. These measures do not guarantee absolute security or confidentiality; no claim of end-to-end encryption is made for bot data. Report suspected exposure privately through a support ticket.
